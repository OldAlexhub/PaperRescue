# Release Notes

## Version 1.0.0 — Initial release

Welcome to PaperRescue!

- Full document scanner with automatic edge detection and perspective correction
- Rescue Scan: multi-frame capture that fuses several photos of the same page into the best possible result
- Manual crop adjustment, rotate, duplicate, and reorder pages
- Original, Enhanced, Grayscale, and Black & White page modes, plus brightness, contrast, sharpening, and noise controls
- On-device OCR — extract, search, and copy recognized text, no internet required
- Instant Scan Quality Score with plain-language feedback and one-tap Rescue Automatically
- Export as PDF (Original / Balanced / Smaller) or JPEG images — no watermark, ever
- Share, save, or open exports in any other app
- Fast local library with search, sort, rename, duplicate, and delete
- Completely free, no account, no subscriptions, no page limits

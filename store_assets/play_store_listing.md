## Full description (max 4000 chars)

**Scan it. Rescue it. PDF it.**

PaperRescue is a full-featured document scanner for Android — completely free, with no page limits, no watermark, no subscriptions, and no account required. Turn receipts, contracts, notes, forms, and books into clean, sharp, searchable PDFs in seconds.

**RESCUE SCAN — our signature feature**
Bad lighting? Shaky hands? Glare from an overhead light? Rescue Scan captures a quick burst of frames of the same page instead of a single photo, then automatically:
• Aligns every frame
• Measures sharpness across the burst
• Detects glare and shadows
• Pulls the clearest, glare-free detail from whichever frame has it
• Corrects perspective and lighting
• Sharpens text and reduces noise

The result is the best possible version of your page — built from real captured detail, never invented or guessed.

**EVERYTHING A PREMIUM SCANNER SHOULD DO**
• Automatic document edge detection & perspective correction
• Auto and manual capture, with manual crop adjustment
• Multi-page scanning — no page limit, ever
• Import photos straight from your gallery
• Reorder, rotate, duplicate, and delete pages
• Original, Enhanced, Grayscale, and Black & White modes
• Brightness, contrast, sharpening, noise reduction
• Background whitening and shadow reduction
• On-device OCR — extract, copy, and search recognized text
• Searchable PDF export where supported
• Export as PDF (Original / Balanced / Smaller file size) or as JPEG images
• Share, save, or open your export in any other app
• Fast local document library with search, sort, rename, duplicate, and delete
• Scan Quality Score with plain-language feedback after every capture

**SCAN QUALITY SCORE**
Every page gets an instant, plain-English quality check — blur, glare, brightness, shadows, perspective, and completeness — with a 0–100 score and clear next steps: Rescue Automatically, Retake, or Keep Anyway. It's an on-device usability estimate, not a lab measurement, but it catches the mistakes that ruin a scan before you move on.

**YOUR DOCUMENTS STAY YOURS**
PaperRescue has no backend. Scanning, enhancement, Rescue Scan processing, and OCR all happen entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. There's no account to create, no login, and no cloud sync — your library lives on your phone, under your control, and you can wipe it at any time from Settings.

**COMPLETELY FREE**
PaperRescue is supported by ads so it can stay free for everyone — no premium tier, no paywalled features, no watermark on your exports, ever.

Scan it. Rescue it. PDF it. — with PaperRescue.

# Privacy Policy for PaperRescue

**Last updated:** September 2026

PaperRescue ("the app") is developed by **Old Alex Hub** ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use PaperRescue: PDF Scanner & OCR.

## The short version

- Everything you scan stays on your device. PaperRescue has no server, no account system, and never uploads your documents anywhere.
- Scanning, perspective correction, image enhancement, Rescue Scan processing, and text recognition (OCR) all run **on-device**.
- The app is free and pays for its development through advertising, using Google's Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob).
- You can delete your local documents at any time, including all at once from Settings.

## What data we collect

**We do not operate a backend and do not collect, receive, or store your documents, page images, thumbnails, extracted text, or quality-analysis results.** All of this data is created and stored exclusively in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Clear Local Data, permanently removes it.

PaperRescue does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use any feature.

## Advertising

PaperRescue is monetized entirely through advertising and has no subscriptions or paid tiers. We use the **Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob)** to show banner ads and, occasionally, full-screen interstitial ads after you finish saving or exporting a document.

To do this, the Google Mobile Ads SDK — and the ad networks Google works with — may collect and process information such as:

- Your advertising ID and other device identifiers
- Approximate (coarse) location, where permitted by your device settings
- App interaction data (e.g., which ads are shown, clicked, or dismissed)
- Diagnostic and performance data
- IP address and general device information (model, OS version, language, time zone)

This information is processed by Google and its advertising partners according to their own privacy policies, not by PaperRescue directly. We do not control what Google's advertising systems do with this data beyond configuring standard AdMob ad units. You can learn more, and manage your ad personalization settings, at:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Google's advertising practices: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
- Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" / "Opt out of Ads Personalization"

The app requests the **INTERNET** permission solely so that ads can be requested and displayed. No other network access occurs.

## Permissions PaperRescue requests

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Camera | Only requested when you tap "Scan Document" or "Rescue Scan." Used to capture document photos. Never activated in the background. |
| Internet | Used only to request and display ads via Google Mobile Ads SDK. |

PaperRescue does **not** request broad photo/storage permissions. Importing photos uses Android's built-in system photo picker (or the Storage Access Framework document picker on older Android versions), which lets you choose specific images without granting the app access to your entire media library. Saving exported files uses Android's "Save As" system picker in the same way.

## On-device processing

The following all run locally on your device using Google's ML Kit (for text recognition) and on-device image-processing libraries — none of it is sent to PaperRescue or any third party as part of these operations:

- Document edge detection and perspective correction
- Rescue Scan multi-frame capture and fusion
- Image enhancement (color, grayscale, black-and-white, brightness, contrast, sharpening, noise reduction, background whitening, shadow reduction)
- Optical character recognition (OCR)
- Scan quality scoring
- PDF and JPEG generation

## Children's privacy

PaperRescue is a general-purpose productivity tool not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because PaperRescue has no account system and no data leaves your device (other than the advertising data flows described above, which are handled by Google per its own policies), the app itself does not collect personal information from any user.

## Data deletion

You are always in control of your local data:

- Delete an individual document or page from the Library or Document Editor screens.
- Delete everything at once from **Settings → Clear Local Data**. This permanently removes every scanned document, page image, thumbnail, and piece of extracted text stored by the app. This action cannot be undone, so export anything you want to keep first.

## Changes to this policy

If we change how PaperRescue works in a way that affects this policy (for example, adding a new feature that changes what data is processed), we will update this document and the "Last updated" date above, and update the in-app copy under Settings → Privacy Policy.
